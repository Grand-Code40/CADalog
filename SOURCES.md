# Company and visual sources

Checked on 29 October 2026. All twelve source service pages were accessed through web retrieval or the browser. This establishes provenance, not quality, pricing, availability or independent endorsement. Original company names and service URLs are preserved.

## Visual policy

- Ten genuine company identifiers (seven logos and three site icons) are referenced at URLs published by the official websites. No stock photos, generated project pictures or fake drawing samples are used.
- Each logo was confirmed loaded on its official page (`complete && naturalWidth > 0`). The three icon URLs and the Praxis logo were also opened directly and checked for decoded image dimensions. Cross-origin loading inside the final local UI still requires browser QA.
- These marks are used narrowly for company identification. Public availability is NOT an open license. No express redistribution permission or open license was found; none is claimed. Files are not copied into the repository, altered or offered for download. External hosting can change or block embedding.
- Failed images fall back to the actual company name as text, never a decorative fake logo or project image. The text is available before/without image loading; requests use `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"`. External hosts still receive the visitor request/IP.
- Global CAD Services has no image: its domain now displays DraftPoint CAD, but the brand relationship was not established. The card visibly explains the discrepancy. SurveysMaps has a text-only identity: no independently loadable logo asset was located in the inspected homepage DOM.
- Project photos/drawing samples are not republished without permission. “Materials on website” opens the official service source, where visitors can inspect examples in their original context.
